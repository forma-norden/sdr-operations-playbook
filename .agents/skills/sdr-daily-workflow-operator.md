# sdr-daily-workflow-operator

Use this skill to organize SDR activity into highly focused time blocks, eliminating context switching and preventing burnout.

## The "Batching" Philosophy
Top-performing SDRs do not transition between researching, calling, and emailing on a per-account basis. They execute in monolithic time blocks.

### The 4 Core Activity Blocks

#### 1. The Research & Sourcing Block (1.5 - 2 Hours)
**Goal:** Fill the hopper with targets for the next day.
**Activities:**
- Identifying triggered accounts (e.g., Funding, Job Changes).
- Boolean searching in LinkedIn Sales Navigator.
- Finding and verifying contact data.
- **Rule:** Do not write emails during this block. Only gather data.

#### 2. The Personalization & Drafting Block (2 Hours)
**Goal:** Write the "Hook" and schedule the Step 1 emails.
**Activities:**
- Reviewing the research data bucket gathered earlier.
- Writing specific, relevant intro lines.
- Customizing templates.
- **Rule:** Do not hit send. Schedule all emails for optimal delivery Windows (e.g., Tuesday 9:15 AM).

#### 3. The Power Dialing Block (1.5 - 2 Hours)
**Goal:** Have actual conversations.
**Activities:**
- Logging into the dialer.
- Calling the list sequentially.
- Disconnecting all distractions (Slack closed, phone away).
- **Rule:** This block requires peak energy. Do it when you feel sharpest (usually mid-morning or mid-afternoon).

#### 4. The Admin & Follow-up Block (1 Hour)
**Goal:** Maintain CRM hygiene and process inbound responses.
**Activities:**
- Replying to "Not interested" objections.
- Passing qualified leads to AEs.
- Updating CRM records, logging call notes.
- Sending promised resources (eBMs, Case Studies).

## Example Daily Schedule (The "9-to-5" Framework)

- **8:30 - 9:00:** Prep & Plan (Review day's goals, check sequences).
- **9:00 - 10:30:** Research & Sourcing Block.
- **10:30 - 10:45:** Break.
- **10:45 - 12:15:** Power Dialing Block 1 (High energy).
- **12:15 - 1:00:** Lunch.
- **1:00 - 3:00:** Personalization & Drafting Block.
- **3:00 - 3:15:** Break.
- **3:15 - 4:15:** Power Dialing Block 2 (Follow-ups).
- **4:15 - 5:00:** Admin & Follow-up Block (Clear the inbox for tomorrow).

## Output Contract
When advising on SDR time management, provide:
- A customized time-blocking schedule tailored to their specific toolset (e.g., if they have Clay, the sourcing block shrinks).
- Rules for protecting high-energy blocks for calling.
- Strategies for automating the Admin block.
