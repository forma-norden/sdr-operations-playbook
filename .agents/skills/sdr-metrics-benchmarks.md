# sdr-metrics-benchmarks

Use this skill to establish KPIs, measure SDR efficiency, and diagnose funnel leaks.
"If you can't measure it, you are just making noise."

## Activity vs. Outcome Metrics

### Core Output KPIs (What matters)
- **Qualified Pipeline Generated ($):** The ultimate North Star.
- **Sales Qualified Leads (SQLs) / Meetings Booked:** The primary yield metric.
- **Meeting Hold Rate:** Target > 80%. (Are the meetings actually occurring?)

### Core Input KPIs (The Lever)
- **Total Accounts Enrolled:** Driven by research/sourcing speed.
- **Dials per Day:** For calling blocks (Target: 40-60 in a 2-hr block).
- **Personalized Emails Sent:** The output of the drafting block (Target: 30-50/day).

## Diagnosing Funnel Leaks

If you are missing your SQL target, look at the conversion rates between steps:

1. **High Activity, Low Connects (Dials -> Conversations < 5%)**
   - *Problem:* Bad phone data, or calling at the wrong times.
   - *Fix:* Switch data providers (e.g., ZoomInfo -> Apollo/Cognism) or shift dial blocks.

2. **High Connects, Low Meetings (Conversations -> Meetings < 3%)**
   - *Problem:* Weak opener, failure to handle objections, pitching features instead of pain.
   - *Fix:* Roleplay objection handling daily. Review call recordings (Gong/Chorus).

3. **High Email Sent, Low Open Rate (< 30%)**
   - *Problem:* Deliverability issues (Going to spam).
   - *Fix:* Stop sending. Audit SPF/DKIM/DMARC. Check domain reputation.

4. **High Open Rate, Low Reply Rate (< 2%)**
   - *Problem:* Weak copy. The premise doesn't resonate or the email is too long.
   - *Fix:* Enforce the Iron Laws of SDR Copy. Shorten emails.

5. **High Meetings Booked, Low Hold Rate (< 60%)**
   - *Problem:* Pushing features, forcing prospects into meetings to hit quota. "Happy Ears."
   - *Fix:* Implement pre-call agenda emails. Stop accepting weak "maybe" commitments on calls.

## Output Contract
When asked to evaluate SDR performance, provide:
- The specific KPI funnel breakdown.
- Identification of the exact stage where the leak is occurring.
- 2 actionable interventions (tactics or process changes) to plug the leak.
