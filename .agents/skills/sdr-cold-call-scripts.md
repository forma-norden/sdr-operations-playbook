# sdr-cold-call-scripts

Use this skill to prepare SDRs for the phones. Focus on specific patterns
for opening the call, diffusing tension, and handling the most common objections.

## The 3-Part Opener 
Never start with "How are you doing today?" (Fake rapport) or "Is now a bad time?" (They will say yes).

### The Pattern-Interrupt Opener
1. **The Name Drop:** "Hey [Name], it's [Your Name] from [Company]."
2. **The Permission (Slightly self-deprecating):** "I know I caught you completely out of the blue. You can absolutely hang up on me, but can I get 30 seconds to tell you why I called, and then you can decide if we keep going?"
3. **The Premise (If they say "Sure"):** "Usually when I talk to [Their Persona], they are pulling their hair out over [Top Pain Point]. Is that something you guys are actively trying to fix right now, or are you actually happy with [Status Quo]?"

## The "Big 3" Objection Handling Frameworks

### Objection 1: "We already use [Competitor]"
- **Bad Response:** "We are better/cheaper."
- **Good Response (The Judo Move):** "I figured you did, [Competitor] is the 800lb gorilla in the space. Most of our best customers started on [Competitor]. Typically, they liked X about it, but got incredibly frustrated with Y as they scaled. Are you guys hitting that Y bottleneck yet?"

### Objection 2: "Just send me an email"
- **Bad Response:** "Okay, what's your email?" (You will never hear from them again).
- **Good Response:** "Happy to, but to make sure I don't send you generic marketing fluff, what specifically should I focus on? The [Feature A] use-case, or the [Feature B] use-case?" (Keeps them talking).

### Objection 3: "We don't have budget right now"
- **Bad Response:** "When does your budget open up?"
- **Good Response:** "Totally fair. Most people I'm talking to right now have their budgets locked tight until Q1. If we pretend money wasn't an issue, is [Pain Point] even a top 3 priority for you to fix this year?"

## Handling Rejection

- If they hang up immediately: Do not follow up. Log it as disqualified.
- If they are rude: Keep an extremely polite tone. "Okay, taking you off the list. Have a good one." Wait 3 months, then sequence a different persona.
- If they say "Call me back in Q3": Immediately say, "No problem. Just so I don't annoy you in Q3, what will have changed between now and then?"

## Output Contract
When advising on call scripts, provide:
- The exact word-for-word script requested.
- The psychological reason *why* the script works (the framing).
- The definition of a "successful" outcome for that specific call (which might just be gathering information, not booking a meeting).
