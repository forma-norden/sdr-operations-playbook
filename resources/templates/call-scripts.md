# Standard SDR Call Scripts

These templates represent the core "baseline" approaches for SDR calls. Do not use these verbatim; adapt them to the specific ICP and toolset.

## 1. The Direct Opener (High Confidence)

Use when dialing high-level executives (VP/C-Level) who value time over rapport.

> "Hi [Name], it's [Your Name] from [Company]. I know I’m calling you out of the blue, and you’re probably busy. Do you have 30 seconds for me to tell you why I called, and then you can tell me if you want to hang up?"
> 
> *[If Yes]*: "Great. Usually when I talk to [Their Persona], they are actively trying to solve [Pain Point 1] or [Pain Point 2]. Are either of those on your radar right now, or are things running smoothly?"

## 2. The Permission-Based Opener (Consultative)

Use when dialing Director/Manager level roles where you want to uncover workflow issues.

> "Hey [Name], [Your Name] with [Company]. I'll be completely upfront, this is a cold call. You can definitely hang up if you need to, but could I get one minute to explain the context?"
>
> *[If Yes]*: "Appreciate it. We’ve been working with [Competitor 1] and [Competitor 2], and they mentioned that as they scaled their [Department], they hit a massive bottleneck with [Specific Task]. I'm curious, how are you currently handling [That Task] today?"

## 3. The Voicemail Script (Goal: Open the Email)

Never pitch on a voicemail. The only goal of a voicemail is to drive attention to an email you are about to send.

> "Hey [Name], it's [Your Name] from [Company]. I have a specific question about how you guys are handling [Pain/Topic]. I know you're busy so no need to call me back. I'm going to send you an email right now with the subject line '[Insert Subject Line]'. Take a look when you have a minute. Thanks."

## 4. The "We Already Use X" Objection Handler (The Judo Move)

When they mention a competitor.

> *Prospect: "We already use Hubspot."*
>
> "I figured you did, Hubspot is the absolute standard for that. Actually, almost all of our best clients use Hubspot alongside us. Usually they love the CRM, but they get frustrated when they try to use it for [Specific gap in Hubspot's product]. Are you guys running into that wall yet, or have you found a workaround?"

## 5. The No-Show Recovery 

When someone misses a scheduled meeting. (Call them exactly 6 minutes after the start time).

> "Hey [Name], it's [Your Name]. We had time on the calendar right now to chat about [Topic], but I figured you got pulled into something urgent. Look, I don't want to chase you down. Should we push this to next week, or is this just totally off the radar right now?" (Give them an easy out).
