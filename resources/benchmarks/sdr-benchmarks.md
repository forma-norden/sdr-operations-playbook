# SDR Performance Benchmarks

Use these industry-standard benchmarks to evaluate SDR team performance. Note: Numbers vary slightly by industry, but these represent healthy B2B SaaS thresholds for ACVs between $15k - $50k.

## Activity Benchmarks (Daily Average)

*Note: These are baseline averages. A rep leveraging heavy automation (e.g. Clay/n8n) will have significantly higher email volume but lower dial volume.*

| Metric | Benchmark | Danger Zone (Action Required) |
|--------|-----------|-----------------------------|
| Total Activities (Calls, Emails, LI) | ~80 - 100 | < 50 |
| Cold Calls (Dials) | 40 - 60 | < 25 |
| Highly Personalized Emails | 25 - 40 | < 15 |
| New Accounts Added to Sequence | 15 - 25 | < 10 |

## Conversion Benchmarks

| Metric | Definition | Benchmark | Excellent |
|--------|------------|-----------|-----------|
| Connect Rate | Standard Dials / Conversations (answered) | 4% - 6% | > 8% |
| Call Conversion | Conversations / Meetings Booked | 3% - 5% | > 7% |
| Email Open Rate | Unique Opens / Delivered | 40% - 50% | > 60% |
| Email Reply Rate | Total Replies / Delivered | 3% - 5% | > 8% |
| Positive Reply Rate | "Yes Let's Chat" / Total Replies | 10% - 15% | > 20% |
| Show/Hold Rate | Meetings Booked / Meetings Occurred | 75% - 85% | > 90% |
| SQO Win Rate | SDR Meetings / Closed Won Deals | 15% - 20% | > 25% |

## Ramp Timelines

Expected timeline to full quota capacity for a net-new SDR hire.

| Month | Expected Quota Attainment | Focus Area |
|-------|---------------------------|------------|
| M1: Onboarding | 0% | Tool stack, product knowledge, ICP certification |
| M2: Building | 25-35% | First dials, building pipeline, shadow calls |
| M3: Ramping | 60-70% | Consistent activity blocks, handling objections |
| M4+ : Fully Ramped | 100%+ | Autonomous execution, territory ownership |
